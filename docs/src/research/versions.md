# Autodrive+ by version

From plugin 0.14 the mod drives the car itself. Each version's notes say what changed, why (usually an in-game drive or the user's report), and what the simulator measured, with what was still untested in game.

- [0.14-0.20](v014.md): the driver itself; how the game answers the controls; the flowing line and road room; traffic, junctions and a faster simulator.
- [0.21](v021.md): a drive on hilly roads.
- [0.22](v022.md): lanes, junctions and overtaking.
- [0.23](v023.md): highway lane drops and the shoulder.
- [0.24](v024.md): the game's vehicle list, and other cars.
- [0.25](v025.md): a fairer simulator, overtaking and braking.
- [0.26](v026.md): driving styles, keeping right, not changing its mind.
- [0.27](v027.md): code layout, the decision log, NPC reactions.
- [0.28](v028.md): our own routes to the map destination.
- [0.29](v029.md): pedals, the game's autodrive HUD, trucks and walkways.
- [0.30](v030.md): grip, steering reach, the driving test, turning round.
- [0.31](v031.md): people on foot, and long vehicles at tight turns.
- [0.31.1](v0311.md): large-vehicle body/ray measurement, steering anticipation and grip.
- [0.31.2](v0312.md): learn the 5-17° / 2 g cornering response, kink guard, plan gradient.
